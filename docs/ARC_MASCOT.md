## Story

In 2018, a real black Schipperke named Ben became an internet meme after
curling into an almost perfect circle. BlueFun's Ben is an original older
character inspired by that simple visual idea, not a copy of the photograph.

Circle built Arc to move internet money, but every circle begins as an arc.
BlueFun's Ben is the old black dog who guarded the unfinished curve. His silver
whiskers mark the years spent waiting for native digital dollars to find their
way home. When Arc opened, Ben curled his tail around the first blue settlement
light and became the final arc in the circle.

BARC represents patience before launch, safe passage between networks and the
belief that serious infrastructure can still have a little character.
